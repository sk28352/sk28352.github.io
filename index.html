Northeastern student support chatbot

Answers international students’ questions about visas, employment, billing, and more from 41 official university documents across 8 categories. Retrieval is filtered to the category a student picks, and every answer links back to the PDFs it came from. It runs fully locally, so no student data leaves the machine.

<30sper answer, with a source citation for every response

LangChain, LLaMA 3.2 (Ollama), ChromaDB, nomic-embed-text, PyPDF, Streamlit

Hybrid transformer mental health chatbot

An empathetic chatbot that works out how someone feels and what they need before replying. RoBERTa detects emotions, Sentence-BERT recognizes intent, and LLaMA 3 writes the response, benchmarked against SVM, Naive Bayes, and LSTM baselines.

0.95emotion detection accuracy, with 0.85 for intent classification

RoBERTa, Sentence-BERT, LLaMA 3, Ollama, PyTorch

Forecasting pipelines on meteorological and turbine data to predict power generation, comparing ARIMA and SARIMA against Prophet, LSTM, and a tuned gradient boosting model, then testing six regressors on turbine output.

Human gait recognition with lightweight MobileNet

Recognizing people by how they walk. We built a new cross-view gait dataset from videos recorded at different speeds and angles, then compared SVM, decision tree, and Inception models against a lightweight MobileNet.
